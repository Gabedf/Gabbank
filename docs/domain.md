# Gabbank Domain

Gabbank is a simplified digital wallet platform where users can store and transfer money within the system. Each user has an associated wallet responsible for maintaining its balance and recording financial operations performed on the platform.

The system allows users to perform deposits, withdrawals, and transfers between wallets. Every financial operation must be recorded in order to maintain transaction history and guarantee traceability of money movement inside the platform.

Transfers represent the main business operation of the system because they involve removing money from one wallet and adding it to another. The platform must guarantee financial consistency, preventing situations such as negative balances, duplicated transactions, or money being lost during operations.

Users may become inactive and wallets may be blocked, preventing financial operations from being executed. The system must also allow users to consult wallet balances and transaction history at any time.

Initially, Gabbank operates only as an internal financial environment, without integration with external banks or payment providers.
